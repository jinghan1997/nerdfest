📢 Call for Applications: Student Travel Grants for portableMRI NerdFest-2

We are excited to announce our Student Travel Grants for portableMRI NerdFest-2! To help make the event more accessible, we are awarding travel support to 3–5 of the most passionate student portableMRI enthusiasts.

🎓 Eligibility

This grant is open to all current students. Both undergraduate and postgraduate students are welcome and encouraged to apply.

🏆 What Do Awardees Receive?

If selected, your grant will include:

  • Free event registration
  • Travel stipend: $1,000 USD for students traveling from non-Asian countries and regions, or $500 USD for students traveling from Asia

🤝 Awardee Duties

By accepting the travel grant, awardees will be expected to collaborate and contribute to the portableMRI NerdFest experience. The selected students will work together to:

  • Form a Student Committee: Collaborate as a team with the other grant recipients.
  • Propose a Mini-Event: Design and propose a specialized mini-event to be featured during the hackathon.
  • Team Presentation: Deliver a three-minute presentation together as a team during the final presentations on Day 4.

📝 How to Apply

Please email your application directly to contact@mrinerdfest.org. Ensure the subject line is exactly: “Apply to student travel grant”.

Your email must include the following two documents:

  1. A two-page CV: Highlight your academic background, projects and relevant experience.
  2. A 500-word write-up: Convince the evaluation committee why you should be selected and explain how attending the event will benefit your studies or research.

Ten applicants will be shortlisted and invited to submit a three-minute video for a second round of evaluation.

📅 Important Dates

  • Application deadline: September 30, 2026
  • Notification of final acceptance: October 15, 2026

🔍 Selection Process

Both rounds of evaluation will be reviewed by the evaluation committee, which is made up of the official portableMRI NerdFest-2 mentors.
